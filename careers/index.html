Operations / Secretary

Are you an organized and detail-oriented individual looking to take the next step in your career? We're currently seeking a motivated Operations/Secretary to join our team at Ironhills Landscaping. In this role, you'll play a crucial part in ensuring the smooth and efficient operation of our business.

Responsibilities:​

  • Assist with day-to-day office tasks, such as answering phones, responding to emails, and maintaining office supplies.

  • Help organize and maintain company records, files, and databases.

  • Assist with special projects and tasks as assigned by management.

Qualifications:

  • High school diploma or equivalent required; associate's or bachelor's degree preferred.

  • Proven experience in an administrative or secretarial role.

  • Strong organizational and multitasking skills, with the ability to prioritize tasks effectively.

  • Excellent communication and interpersonal skills.

  • Proficiency in Microsoft Office Suite and other relevant software applications.

At Ironhills Landscaping, we value teamwork, professionalism, and a commitment to excellence. If you're ready to take on a challenging and rewarding role in a dynamic work environment, we want to hear from you! Apply today to join our team as an Operations/Secretary.
