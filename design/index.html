Design Your Dream Landscape

At Ironhills Landscaping we believe that every great outdoor space begins with a clear vision. That's why we offer comprehensive design services to help you bring your landscaping dreams to life, regardless of whether you choose to build with us or not.

​

Our experienced team of designers will work closely with you to understand your preferences, lifestyle, and budget, translating your ideas into stunning 2D and 3D renderings that showcase the potential of your outdoor space. With meticulous attention to detail and a commitment to creativity, we'll create a custom design that reflects your unique style and enhances the beauty and functionality of your property.

​

But here's the best part – you don't have to commit to building with us to benefit from our design services. Whether you decide to proceed with construction on your own or choose another contractor, you'll still have access to our expert design team to guide you through the process.

​

However, if you do decide to build with us, there's an added incentive. A portion of the design fee will be applied towards your landscaping project, making it even more affordable to bring your vision to life with us.

​

GET STARTED NOW (DESIGN DESCOVERY)

​

Your design starts here in 5 easy steps

​

​

1 - (Design Discovery Call) First, we will schedule a discovery call to discuss your vision and listen to your needs, requirements and budget for outdoor living space.

 

2 - (Proposal and Deposit) After our call, we will then send you a design proposal based on your new project along with a deposit amount to secure our working with you.

 

3 - (Site Survey and Inspiration gather) After you submit your deposit, we will schedule a face to face meeting to gather photos, measurements of your space so that we can start the design process to bring your vision to life.

 

4 - (Face to face/Screen share, revisions) After we've created the first concept, we will meet live and screen share to walk through what we've come up with and get your feedback while making live changes and notes.

 

5 - (Design Package) Once things are finalized, we will send your new completed design via email with visual images, build plan, walkthrough video link, and everything you need to make your design smoothly become a reality.
