Water & Fire Features

Water Features

  • Waterfalls:

    • Create a captivating focal point with cascading water.

    • Add drama and sound to your landscape for a soothing ambiance.

    • Available in various styles and sizes to suit your preferences.

  • Streams:

    • Meandering waterways that mimic the flow of natural rivers.

    • Enhance the tranquility of your yard with gentle babbling sounds.

    • Can be lined with rocks and plants for a naturalistic look.

  • Water Walls:

    • Vertical features with water cascading down a smooth surface.

    • Provide a striking visual effect and a sense of modern elegance.

    • Made of materials like glass, metal, or stone for customization options.

  • Ponds:

    • Create a serene oasis with a tranquil body of water.

    • Ideal for supporting aquatic life and incorporating water plants.

    • Offer endless possibilities for landscaping around the pond's edge.

  • Water Features with Fire:

    • Combine the mesmerizing elements of water and fire.

    • Create a captivating contrast and add warmth to your outdoor space.

    • Available in designs such as fire bowls or fire pits integrated with water features.

Fire Features

​

  • Fire Pits:

    • Versatile and popular, fire pits provide a focal point for gatherings and create a cozy ambiance.

  • Fire Bowls:

    • Stylish and modern, fire bowls offer a contemporary twist on traditional fire features, adding a touch of elegance to your outdoor setting.

  • Fire Tables:

    • Combining functionality with style, fire tables provide a practical surface for dining or entertaining while also offering the warmth and ambiance of a fire feature.

  • Outdoor Fireplaces:

    • Ideal for creating a focal point in larger outdoor spaces, outdoor fireplaces offer a grand and inviting atmosphere for gatherings and relaxation.

  • Built-in Fire Features:

    • Seamlessly integrated into your landscape design, built-in fire features blend harmoniously with their surroundings, enhancing the overall aesthetic of your outdoor space.

  • Custom Fire Features:

    • Tailored to your specific preferences and requirements, custom fire features allow you to design a unique and personalized focal point for your outdoor area.

  • Gas Fire Features:

    • Clean-burning and convenient, gas fire features provide instant warmth and ambiance at the flick of a switch, with minimal maintenance required.

  • Wood-Burning Fire Features:

    • Offering a traditional and rustic charm, wood-burning fire features create an authentic outdoor experience, complete with crackling flames and the scent of burning wood.

  • Portable Fire Features:

    • Perfect for versatility and convenience, portable fire features can be easily moved to different areas of your outdoor space, allowing you to create ambiance wherever you desire.
